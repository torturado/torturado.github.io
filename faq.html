What is EXP Bank?

EXP Bank is the gem-deposit system modeled by this calculator. The tool estimates how a starting gem balance grows at a fixed 0.30% daily compound interest rate. It also estimates time to a target balance and Discord rank milestones.

How is EXP Bank gem interest calculated?

The formula is FV = PV × (1 + r)^t, where FV is your future balance, PV is your current gems, r is 0.003 (the 0.30% daily rate), and t is the duration in days. Interest compounds: each period grows the balance including the interest already earned.

For example, 1,000 gems become approximately 1,094.03 gems after 30 days, a profit of 94.03 gems. See the gem growth examples for other durations.

How long does it take to double your gems?

At 0.30% daily compound interest, doubling takes about 231.4 days, using t = ln(2) / ln(1.003). For example, a balance of 1,000 gems reaches 2,000 gems after that time in the calculator's model. If you count only complete days, the balance first exceeds double after 232 days.

How do I use Goal Gems and Additional Gems?

Enter your starting balance in Current Gems. Use Goal Gems for the total balance you want to reach, or Additional Gems for the extra amount you want to earn. Then select Calculate to see the estimated time to each target.

With 1,000 current gems, Goal Gems of 2,000 and Additional Gems of 1,000 describe the same target. Additional Gems is a profit goal, not a new deposit. Rank presets let you select a Discord milestone as your goal.

What is the difference between a time period and a target date?

A time period specifies how long your gems grow, using years, months, days, hours, minutes, or seconds. The calculator treats one year as 365 days and one month as 30 days. A Target Date and optional Target Time use the interval from now to that calendar date instead.

Shorter periods use an equivalent hourly rate derived from the daily rate: (1.003)^(1/24) − 1.

How accurate is the EXP calculator?

The calculator uses high-precision arithmetic to handle large gem balances. Displayed values are rounded, and results depend on the fixed 0.30% daily rate and the chosen duration. They are estimates; the EXP Bank service may apply different rounding or crediting rules.

Can I calculate multiple deposits or withdrawals?

Each calculation models one starting balance without later transactions. To estimate a later deposit or withdrawal, calculate growth up to that point, adjust the resulting balance, and use it as Current Gems for the next period. Repeat for each transaction.

Are there deposit limits, withdrawal fees, or rate changes?

The calculator accepts a positive starting balance and uses a fixed 0.30% daily rate. It does not check account eligibility, deposit limits, withdrawal fees, or changes to the bank's rules. Check those details directly with the EXP Bank service.

How can I report a calculator issue?

Use the contact page or the GitHub issue tracker. Include the inputs, the result you received, and what you expected so the calculation can be reproduced.
