How to use the EXP calculator

  1. Enter your balance in Current Gems.
  2. Choose a time period in years, months, days, hours, minutes, or seconds, or enter a Target Date and optional time.
  3. Optionally set Goal Gems for a target total, or Additional Gems for the extra amount you want to earn.
  4. Select Calculate to see your projected balance, profit, growth percentage, and time to your goal.

For example, with 1,000 current gems, a goal of 2,000 gems and an additional-gems target of 1,000 both mean earning another 1,000 gems. Additional Gems is a profit target; it does not add a new deposit.

How EXP Bank compound interest is calculated

The gem calculator uses FV = PV × (1 + r)^t. FV is the future balance, PV is your starting balance, r is the daily rate of 0.003, and t is the duration in days. Profit is the future balance minus your starting balance.

For 1,000 gems over 30 days: 1,000 × 1.003^30 ≈ 1,094.03 gems. Interest compounds because each period grows the balance including previously earned interest.

One year is treated as 365 days and one month as 30 days. Shorter periods use the equivalent hourly rate, (1.003)^(1/24) − 1. Calculations run in your browser with high-precision arithmetic. Results are projections based on this fixed rate.
